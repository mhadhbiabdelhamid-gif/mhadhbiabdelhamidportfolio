About

How I work

I have spent my career close to the operation rather than above it — the broadcast that has to go out on time, the stock count that has to match, the supplier who has to be paid this week, the listing that has to be live in both languages before the weekend. That is where I learned to keep records that hold up, chase the detail nobody else is tracking, and spot the step that should have been automated three months ago. Everything I run is bilingual from the first document rather than translated afterwards, and anything automated stops for a person before it touches a live record.
